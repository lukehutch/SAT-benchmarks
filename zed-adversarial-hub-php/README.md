# Adversarial Bit-Vector Hub & Pigeonhole Principle Circuits (`zed-adversarial-hub-php`)

## Overview & Origin

An 18-instance adversarial stress-audit and scaling suite combining two families originally constructed as bit-level AIGs (and exported to flat DIMACS CNF/XCNF):

1. **Bit-Vector Hub Arithmetic Disjunctions (`hub_*`, 6 instances):** A 10-bit shared hub integer $h \in [\text{low}, 63]$ coupled to $n \in \{20, 24, 30, 60, 80\}$ pairs of 10-bit integer variables $(x_i, y_i)$ via bit-blasted two's-complement adder/multiplier chains enforcing $(-x_i - 2h + 40 + (i \bmod 7) \ge 0) \lor (y_i - h - 10 + (i \bmod 5) \ge 0)$ (`low = 31` is UNSAT; `low = 25` is SAT). Designed to stress-test soundness under high shared-hub fanout.
2. **Pigeonhole Principle Circuits (`php_*`, 12 instances):** Tseitin-encoded $\mathrm{PHP}^{m}_{n}$ circuits placing $m$ pigeons into $n$ holes, spanning UNSAT instances $\mathrm{PHP}^{9}_{8}$ through $\mathrm{PHP}^{50}_{49}$ (`64,875` CNF vars, `247,350` clauses) plus satisfiable bijection controls $\mathrm{PHP}^{20}_{20}$ and $\mathrm{PHP}^{30}_{30}$.

- **Origin:** **Original** (MIT [`../LICENSE`](../LICENSE)) — bit-vector hub disjunction circuits (`hub_*`) and Pigeonhole Principle circuits (`php_*`, following Haken 1985) generated by the standalone Rust generator in `src/`.
- **Why they are hard:** Haken (1985) proved every pure Resolution / CDCL refutation of $\mathrm{PHP}^{n+1}_{n}$ requires $2^{\Omega(n)}$ steps. [Kissat](https://github.com/arminbiere/kissat) 4.0.4, [CaDiCaL](https://github.com/arminbiere/cadical) 3.1.0, and [CryptoMiniSat](https://github.com/msoos/cryptominisat) 5.18.0 all time out past $600\;\text{s}$ by $\mathrm{PHP}^{16}_{15}$.

## Directory Layout & Regeneration

- `aig-orig/` — Original native **Binary AIGER 1.9** circuits (`254.2 KB`, verified with [Berkeley ABC](https://github.com/berkeley-abc/abc)).
- `cnf-converted/` — Tseitin **DIMACS `.cnf.xz`** conversions (`746.6 KB`).
- `xcnf-converted/` — Native-XOR **[CryptoMiniSat](https://github.com/msoos/cryptominisat) `.xcnf.xz`** conversions (`691.6 KB`).
- `anf-converted/` — Lossless linear-size **DIMACS-ANF `.anf.xz`** conversions (`418.4 KB`, degree $\le 2$, generated via [`../src/to_anf.py`](../src/to_anf.py)).
- `src/` — Standalone zero-dependency Rust generator crate (`cd src && cargo run --release`).
